Acme Robotics Employee Handbook (Sample)

Section 1: Leave Policy
Full-time employees at Acme Robotics accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO)
per calendar year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month
of service. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next
calendar year; any remaining balance above 5 days is forfeited on
December 31st. Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal
at least 5 business days in advance for planned leave.

Section 2: Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager
approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are
reviewed every 6 months. All remote employees must be reachable during
core hours, which are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone.

Section 3: Expense Reimbursement
Business-related expenses under $75 do not require pre-approval and can
be submitted directly through the expense system with a receipt attached.
Expenses of $75 or more require manager pre-approval before the expense
is incurred. Reimbursement is typically processed within 10 business days
of submission.

Section 4: Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December.
Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment, and a
calibration step across the team. Compensation changes resulting from
reviews take effect in the following pay cycle.

Section 5: Equipment Policy
New employees receive a standard equipment kit consisting of a laptop,
monitor, keyboard, and mouse within their first week. Equipment requests
outside the standard kit (e.g., a second monitor, specialized software)
must be submitted to IT with manager approval and are reviewed weekly.
